Compute Campus Hotline

Ask a question. Share what you know.

Questions or tips about Arkansas compute campuses are welcome from residents, officials, workers, and reporters. You can leave your name and email blank if you want to stay anonymous on the form.

Be honest about anonymity: choosing anonymous means we will not have your name or email to contact you. It is not a legal shield. Do not send anything you are not allowed to share. We never ask for passwords, payment details, or ID numbers.

Only if you'd like a private reply.

What happens next

  1. We read every submission.
  2. We check tips and claims against public records before anything is published.
  3. We do not publish accusations without a documented public record.
  4. Important questions get a plain-English answer on Public Answers, with sources, so everyone can read them.
  5. Anonymous submissions cannot get a private reply.

Reporters and news outlets

Use this form for media questions, or contact us through greyswancapital.ai/contact. Include your outlet if you want a reply. You're welcome to cite facility pages and the players report; please link the page and the date updated.
