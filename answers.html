How Public Answers works

Important questions from the Compute Campus Hotline are researched here and answered for everyone. Each posted answer will show:

  • the question (identifying details removed)
  • a plain-English answer
  • what is known vs. not yet public
  • linked sources
  • the related facility page, when there is one
  • the date answered (Central Time)

Tips and claims are checked against public records before anything is published. We do not publish accusations without a documented record. Names and identifying details are never published without permission.
